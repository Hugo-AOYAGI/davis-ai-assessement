The study was accepted by the gate.

**Site:** parcel 91272000CR0247, 4 rue Joliot-Curie, 5,951.6 m².

**Zone and overlays:**
- The zone is UMG (Joliot-Curie mixed quarter).
- Two legend items apply: "Limites de zones" and the OAP sector.
- The OAP (chapter 8) is a compatibility document, not strict conformity.

**Verdict per building:**
- **Résidence Sociale Étudiante:** permitted. The zone is meant for student housing.
- **Résidence Jeunes Actifs:** permitted, but the PLU does not define it. I treated it as housing, and this is the weaker of the two classifications.

**Envelope figures from the gate:**
- Buildable footprint after setbacks: 4,241.4 m². This is also the maximum footprint, because the PLU sets no footprint ratio.
- Minimum green area: 1,190.3 m² (20% of the plot, including 10% pleine terre).
- Maximum height: 20 m (R+5, 6 storeys). The OAP aims at an average of R+4.
- Maximum volume: 84,828.8 m³.
- Setbacks: road at least 1 m. Side and rear 6 m facing views, 3 m without. I used 6 m.
- The two buildings must be at least 10 m apart if either facing facade has views, otherwise 6 m.

**Parking:**
- **Résidence Sociale Étudiante:** 1 space per 150 m² of floor area.
- **Résidence Jeunes Actifs:** 1 space per 35 m² of floor area, with a maximum of 2 per dwelling. If the dwellings are State-aided, it is 1 per dwelling. A hotel-type reading would give 0.5 per room.

**Main ambiguities and my choices:**
- **Edge labelling:** the gate found no site edge facing public land and labelled all 16 edges as rear limits. The southern edge probably fronts rue Joliot-Curie, where only 1 m is required. The envelope is therefore conservative on that side.
- **OAP siting:** the OAP puts student housing mainly in the west of the district. I read this as an orientation, not a bar to this location.
- **Classification:** I classed both residences as housing for height and parking. Confirm this with the planning authority.
- **OAP map position:** the OAP maps are schematic. I assumed the parcel is not on a public-space symbol, and this needs checking on a larger-scale document.

No floor areas, unit counts or financing were given, so the parking figures are ratios only. The PLU prohibits derogations.
