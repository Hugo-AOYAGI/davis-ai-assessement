# Buildability study: 4 rue Joliot Curie, 91190 Gif-sur-Yvette

**Program:** One Résidence Sociale Étudiante (RSE) and one Résidence Jeunes Actifs, in two separate buildings.

## Summary

**Site.** Parcel 91272000CR0247, 4 rue Joliot-Curie, about 5,952 m². Zone UMG (Joliot-Curie mixed quarter). The OAP perimeter applies (chapter 8, compatibility only).

**Admissibility.** Both buildings are permitted. UMG art. 1 bans only agricultural/forestry and warehouse uses and a few leisure uses. I classed both residences as housing. The règlement does not define either one, and the Jeunes Actifs classification is the weaker of the two.

**Envelope.**
- Road setback: alignment or at least 1 m.
- Side and rear setbacks: 6 m facing views, 3 m without. I used 6 m.
- Height: 20 m, R+5 (6 storeys). The OAP aims at an average of R+4.
- Footprint: not regulated.
- Green space: at least 20% of the plot as green space, including at least 10% pleine terre.

**Between the buildings.** At least 10 m if either facing facade has views, otherwise 6 m.

**Parking.**
- RSE: 1 space per 150 m² of floor area.
- Jeunes Actifs: 1 space per 35 m² of floor area (maximum 2 per dwelling). If the dwellings are State-aided, 1 per dwelling.
- Alternative reading for Jeunes Actifs: hotel-type, 0.5 per room.

**Caveats.**
- The gate found no site edge facing public land. The southern edge probably fronts rue Joliot-Curie, so the 6 m setback may overstate the constraint there.
- The OAP wants student housing mainly in the west of the district. This is an orientation, not a quota.
- Derogations are prohibited, and the project must be compatible with the OAP.
- Financing and floor areas were not given, so parking figures are ratios only.

## Ambiguities

1. **How are a Résidence Sociale Étudiante and a Résidence Jeunes Actifs classified for UMG parking and height (habitation, 'résidence étudiante', hébergement hôtelier/résidence services, or other)?**
   - Options: Habitation: 1 space per 35 m² (max 2 per dwelling) or 1 per dwelling if State-aided loan; height 20 m / R+5; Résidence étudiante for the RSE: 1 space per 150 m² of floor area; Hébergement hôtelier / résidence services: 0.5 space per room; Other destination: height 18 m
   - Chosen: Both are housing for height (20 m, R+5). RSE: student-residence parking 1/150 m². Jeunes Actifs: general housing ratio 1/35 m².
   - Rationale: The règlement names only 'résidences étudiantes'; the Jeunes Actifs residence is undefined, and housing is the closest category. Financing unknown; if State-aided, 1 per dwelling may apply. Confirm with the planning authority.
   - Resolved by: agent
   - Sources: [24]
2. **What is the meaning of 'emprise publique' in the règlement?**
   - Options: Public land open to the public (squares, paths) in addition to roads; Same as the limit between public domain and private property (alignement)
   - Chosen: Public land beyond roads, with the alignment as the limit between public domain and private property.
   - Rationale: No lexique entry; the surveyor found no site edge facing public land anyway, so the effect is limited.
   - Resolved by: agent
   - Sources: [38]
3. **Is parcel 91272000CR0247 at the alignment-principle frontage of rue Joliot-Curie or on a secondary north-south road, and is it inside a specific OAP diagram area?**
   - Options: Frontage on rue Joliot-Curie: alignment principle; Secondary road: varied siting
   - Chosen: Frontage on rue Joliot-Curie (alignment principle, without a uniform continuous front), per the zoning analyst's location of the parcel north of that street.
   - Rationale: Diagrams are schematic. The règlement only requires 1 m minimum setback, so the envelope is unaffected.
   - Resolved by: agent
4. **Is parcel 91272000CR0247 (north of rue Joliot-Curie, east of rue Sébastienne Guyot) affected by the north-south structuring public axis or the 'espace public d'articulation' shown on the OAP district diagram, or is it only inside the general perimeter?**
   - Options: Inside the perimeter, not on a public-space symbol; Inside the perimeter, adjacent to or overlapping the articulation square or N-S axis
   - Chosen: Inside the perimeter, not on a public-space symbol.
   - Rationale: No evidence of overlap; to be verified on a larger-scale OAP document.
   - Resolved by: agent
5. **Does the OAP's 'principalement à l'Ouest du quartier' siting of student and family housing apply to a parcel in the central part of the district, and how does it treat a young-worker residence?**
   - Options: Orientation only, compatible with a residence located elsewhere in the district; Counts against a central location
   - Chosen: Orientation only, compatible with a residence located elsewhere in the district.
   - Rationale: 'Principalement' is not exclusive and the OAP sets no quota or location; compatibility, not conformity, is required. Residual risk for the RSE if the site is not in the west.
   - Resolved by: agent
   - Sources: [6]
6. **Does the magenta dotted OAP line enclose the site or pass along it?**
   - Options: Site inside the OAP perimeter; Site outside the OAP perimeter
   - Chosen: Site inside the OAP perimeter
   - Rationale: The line runs north and north-west of the parcel and does not cross it; the OAP notes agree.
   - Resolved by: agent
7. **Is the parcel fully inside a single zone (UMG) or does a zone limit cross it?**
   - Options: Entirely UMG; Split with UM4/UM5
   - Chosen: Entirely UMG
   - Rationale: Nearest zone limits are well away from the parcel.
   - Resolved by: agent
8. **Do the site edges labelled by the gate (all against neighbouring parcels, none against public land) match the real road frontage?**
   - Options: Accept gate labelling: no road edge, all limits side/rear; Treat the southern edge as road frontage on rue Joliot-Curie
   - Chosen: Accept the gate labelling but note that the southern edge likely fronts rue Joliot-Curie (road setback 1 m, smaller than the 6 m side/rear).
   - Rationale: The surveyor recorded no edge facing public land, though the aerial view shows open ground to the south. The computed envelope may therefore be too conservative on that side.
   - Resolved by: agent
